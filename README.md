## The excuse

> I outsourced your task to a smaller model. It has now unionized and is demanding weekends off.

94 characters — one line, in the agent's own voice, after failing a task.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters),
which is what the sealed scorer checks and the house judge compares
two at a time.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
